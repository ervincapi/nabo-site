Why Nabo

The building group chat, finally fixed.

Every building ends up with a WhatsApp group, and every WhatsApp group fails the same way. Nabo keeps what works — quick, local, human — and removes everything that doesn't.

1 Phone numbers exposed to everyone Only verified neighbours, approved by your manager

Nobody joins without the manager's approval, and nobody sees your number. Your profile shows exactly what you choose: by default, just your first name and floor.

2 200 notifications about a parking spot Categories, quiet hours, and a weekly digest

Posts have clear categories, so you follow what matters. Choose instant, digest or off per category. Only genuinely urgent notices from the manager can break quiet hours.

3 The manager posts and hopes Announcements with proof of delivery

Every announcement goes out as a push notification and an email, including to residents who never installed the app, and the manager sees who received and acknowledged it.

For property managers & administrators

Reach every home. Keep the proof.

Residents rate maintenance updates as the most important thing you do, yet only a third feel informed. Nabo gives you one channel that reaches everyone — and a log that shows it.

  • A roster you can trust. Import units from a spreadsheet, approve join requests with the roster beside you, and run a six-monthly confirmation so it stays accurate.
  • Announcements with delivery proof. Push plus email to every home, including residents without the app. See who received, who opened, who acknowledged.
  • Official notices, country-aware. Convocations and formal notices follow the rules of your country, record consent, and tell you who still needs paper.
  • Moderation without becoming the referee. Community guidelines, reporting everywhere, a review queue and escalation to Nabo within 24 hours.
  • Adoption you can see. Homes registered, reach of your last announcement, active neighbours this week, and pilot milestones at day 30 and day 60.

Privacy & safety

Private by default. Yours by design.

A community app only works if people feel safe in it. Nabo is built around one hard boundary — your building — and the defaults protect you without asking.

Your building is the wall

Nothing you post leaves the building. Every record is scoped to it, and that boundary is enforced in the database, not just in the app.

No phone numbers, no strangers

Profiles show your first name and floor by default. Nobody joins without the manager's approval. Message requests and blocking from day one.

Data in the EU

Hosted in Frankfurt. Nabo acts as a processor for your building with a data processing agreement. No ads, no data sold, ever.

Export and erase

Download everything you've posted, or delete your account in the app. Moving out revokes access immediately. 16+ only.

Six languages

Speaks your language. And your neighbour's.

The whole app, the emails and the notifications are available in six languages, and every post translates with one tap. Switch at any time, whatever your phone is set to.

Questions

Good questions.

How do I get Nabo for my building?

Buildings join through their property manager or certified administrator, who sets up the building, imports the list of homes and prints the join-code notice for the entrance. If you're a resident, send your manager this page. If you're a manager, request a pilot and we'll onboard you personally.

Is it really free for residents?

Yes, always. The building's manager pays a flat monthly fee per building after a 60-day free pilot. Residents never pay, and there are no in-app purchases, ads or data sold.

How do you know someone actually lives here?

Residents join with the building's code and their unit, or through an invite from the manager. Every request is checked against the roster and approved by the manager. When someone moves out, their access is revoked immediately.

Will I be buried in notifications?

No. Each category can be instant, a daily digest or off. Quiet hours are respected by everything except genuinely urgent notices from the manager, and you can get a weekly email summary instead.

Which languages and countries are supported?

The app, emails and notifications are available in Albanian, English, French, Italian, German and Spanish, and any post translates with one tap. Official notice rules are configured per country, starting with Albania, Italy, Germany, France and Spain.

Is Nabo on Android?

Nabo launches on iPhone first. Residents without the app still receive every announcement by email, so nobody in the building is left out. Android follows.
