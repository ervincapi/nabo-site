Nabo uses the following companies to process personal data. Building data is stored in the European Union. Where a company processes data outside the EU or EEA, the transfer is covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs). For Albania, transfers rely on the Commissioner's list of adequate countries or on equivalent clauses, with a documented risk assessment.
