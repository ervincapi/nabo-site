Community guidelines

Applies to every building · last updated 9 November 2026

These guidelines are shown to every resident before their first post, in the six languages Nabo supports.

Be a good neighbour

Write the way you would talk in the stairwell. Disagree with ideas, not people. Assume good intent; most problems in a building are misunderstandings.

Keep it about the building

Questions, notices, recommendations, lost and found, events, things to lend or give away. Not politics, not national news, not advertising for businesses outside the building.

Respect privacy

Do not post other people's phone numbers, photos, or details about who is away from home. Do not photograph neighbours or their doors without asking. What is said in the building stays in the building.

No harassment, no discrimination

No insults, threats, or content that targets anyone for who they are. Repeated unwanted contact is harassment, even when each message seems harmless.

No scams, no sales

The lend & borrow board is for lending and giving, not selling. Never ask for payments or deposits. Report anything that looks like a scam.

Reporting and moderation

Every post, comment and message has a report button. Reports go to your building's moderators; content with three independent reports is hidden automatically pending review. Unresolved reports escalate to Nabo within 24 hours. Every decision comes with a reason, and you can appeal it.

Managers and moderators

Managers and moderators follow the same rules and are held to a higher standard. They may pin, hide or remove content and deactivate memberships, and each action is logged.

Contact

Something urgent or sensitive? Write to info@nabo-app.org.
