Terms of service

Draft · applies to the pilot programme · last updated 9 November 2026

The full terms in Albanian, English, French, Italian, German and Spanish are being finalised. Until they are published, pilot buildings are covered by the pilot agreement signed with their property manager, together with the summary below.

What Nabo is

Nabo is a private community service for a single apartment building, provided to the building through its property manager or certified administrator (the customer). Residents use Nabo free of charge, once approved by the customer.

Accounts

You sign in with your email address; there is no password. You must be at least 16 years old. You are responsible for keeping your email account secure and for everything posted from your account. You can see your active devices and sign them out in the app.

Your content

You keep ownership of what you post. You grant Nabo the licence needed to store, display and, at your request, translate it for the other members of your building. Content never leaves the building it was posted in. You agree to follow the community guidelines; content that breaks them can be hidden or removed, and you can appeal every decision.

Lending and borrowing

The lend & borrow board connects neighbours; Nabo is not a party to any loan, and no payments or deposits are handled in the app. Lenders decide who borrows and remain responsible for their items.

Billing

Residents never pay. The customer pays a flat monthly fee per building after a 60-day free pilot, billed on the web by card or SEPA direct debit, and may cancel at any time at the end of a billing period. There are no in-app purchases.

Ending your use

You can delete your account from the app at any time. When you move out, the customer deactivates your membership and your access ends immediately; your posts remain visible to the building unless you delete them first.

Liability and law

Nabo is provided with reasonable care but without warranty of uninterrupted availability. To the extent permitted by law, liability is limited to the fees paid by the customer for the building in the preceding twelve months. Mandatory consumer rights in your country are not affected.
