Privacy policy

Draft · last updated 9 November 2026

The full privacy policy in six languages, the data processing agreement for buildings and the sub-processor list are being finalised. The summary below describes how Nabo already works.

Who is responsible

For a building's community, the property manager or administrator is the data controller and Nabo acts as the processor under a data processing agreement. For your account itself (email address, sessions, language), Nabo is the controller.

What we collect

  • Account: email address, display name, preferred language, devices and sessions.
  • Membership: building, unit and role, as confirmed by the building's manager.
  • Content: posts, comments, reactions, events, listings and messages you create, and any images you attach.
  • Delivery: for announcements, whether each recipient received, opened and acknowledged them. Managers need this to prove that notices reached residents.
  • Technical: crash reports and anonymous usage statistics, with IP addresses truncated.

What we do not do

No advertising, no selling or sharing of data for marketing, no tracking across other apps or sites, no reading of your messages for anything other than delivering them and acting on reports you or others make.

Where data lives

All data is stored in Frankfurt, Germany, in the European Union. Transactional emails are sent by an email provider; post translations are produced on demand by a translation provider that does not retain the text. A full sub-processor list will be published here.

How long we keep it

Content stays while your building uses Nabo. Delivery logs for official notices are kept for the period required by the building's country. When a building leaves Nabo, its data is deleted within 90 days. Deleting your account removes your personal data and anonymises your past posts.

Your rights

You can export everything you have posted and delete your account directly in the app. You can also ask us to access, correct, restrict or object to processing of your data, and you have the right to complain to your national data protection authority.

Age

Nabo is for people aged 16 and over.

Contact

Privacy questions and requests: info@nabo-app.org.
